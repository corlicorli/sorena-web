Sorena shows no data

Sorena needs permission to read Apple Health. Open the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps, then Sorena, and turn on the categories. On iOS 27, share all recorded data rather than the last 30 days, so Sorena can build your recovery baseline.

Today says "Waiting for data"

Your recovery score needs last night's sleep and heart data. Wear your Apple Watch to bed and open Sorena after it has synced in the morning. Scores settle over the first few days as Sorena learns your normal.

Using Sorena on Apple Watch

Install the Watch app from the Watch app on your iPhone. Start a workout on either device and it mirrors to the other, with heart rate and calories from your Watch.

Changing the language

Sorena follows your iPhone's language. To choose English or Danish yourself, go to Profile, then Language in Sorena. The change applies the next time you open the app.

Deleting your data

In Sorena, go to Profile, then Account, then Erase all data. This removes everything Sorena keeps on your iPhone. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Apple Watch is optional and makes recovery scores much better.
