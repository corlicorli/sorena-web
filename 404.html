Rest is part of the plan

Most training apps count. Sorena listens. Your watch already knows how you slept, how your heart recovered and how hard yesterday was. What it doesn't do is tell you what that means for today. Sorena does, once, every morning.

One answer, not twenty numbers

Every morning you get a single decision: train hard, train normally or take it easy. The numbers are there when you want them, each with the reasons that moved it. You should never need a spreadsheet to know how to feel.

Your normal, not the average

A good night for you is not a good night for someone else. Sorena compares you with your own last 90 days, so a score means something the first time you read it and more every week after.

A plan that bends

Hard days build you and easy days let you keep building. After a short night the weights ease off a little, and when you are ready the plan asks for more. You still train either way.

Yours, and only yours

Your data stays on your iPhone and in Apple Health. No account is needed, nothing is sold, and there are no ads or analytics. We never see your health data, and we built it that way on purpose.

Quiet by design

Sorena speaks when it has something to say. A note in the morning, your session when you start it, no noise around it. The best training app is the one you barely notice.
