Privacy policy

Updated 2 November 2026

Who is responsible

Sorena is made in Denmark ("Sorena", "we"). We are the data controller for the processing described under Sign in with Apple. You can reach us at kontakt@canf.dk.

What stays on your iPhone

Your training plan, workouts, sets, notes, goals, body stats, injuries, settings and profile photo are stored only in Sorena's storage on your iPhone. The profile photo is the one picture you choose; Sorena can't see the rest of your photo library. We run no server that holds this data, and we can't see it. Like other app data, it is included in your iPhone backups if you have them turned on.

Ask Sorena

Ask Sorena runs on your iPhone with Apple's on-device model. Your questions, its replies and the data it reads are never sent to us or to any AI service in the cloud. Your chats, notes, goals and plan changes are kept in a protected file on your iPhone and are removed when you erase your data. Its replies are not medical advice; see the terms.

Apple Health

With your permission, Sorena reads from Apple Health:

  • sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting and walking heart rate, breathing rate, wrist temperature, blood oxygen and VO2 max
  • workouts and their routes, workout effort, steps, active energy and walking, running, cycling and swimming distances
  • body weight, body fat, dietary energy and protein

It writes to Apple Health the workouts you finish or log (with their energy, distance and effort), and the body weight, body fat, calories and protein you enter.

Health data is used only to show your recovery, training and progress inside the app. It is never sent to us or anyone else, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared or sold. You can change Sorena's access at any time in the Health app under Sharing, then Apps.

Apple Watch

The Watch app sends your live workout directly between your Apple Watch and iPhone. It doesn't pass through any server of ours.

Sign in with Apple

Sorena works fully without an account. Where the app offers Sign in with Apple and you choose to use it, Apple shares a user identifier and, the first time, the name and email you allow (this can be a private relay address). Sorena keeps these only on your iPhone, in its storage and the Keychain, and uses the name to fill in your profile.

If you delete your account, the app asks Apple for a one-time code and sends it to a small server function we run on Supabase. That function passes the code to Apple to revoke Sorena's sign-in, as Apple requires, and stores nothing. Like any web server, the provider may briefly log technical details such as your IP address.

Maps and notifications

Workout route maps use Apple Maps, so the map images for that area are loaded from Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Reminders and morning notifications are scheduled on your iPhone; no push server is involved.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising code from third parties.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data brokers.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data.

Legal basis

Health data is processed on your device only with your explicit consent, which you give in the Apple Health permission screen and can withdraw there at any time (GDPR article 9(2)(a)). Revoking Sign in with Apple when you delete your account is done to carry out your request and meet Apple's rules (article 6(1)(b) and (f)).

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, export and delete your data, to withdraw consent and to object. Because your data is on your iPhone, you can do most of this yourself in the app and the Health app. For anything else, contact us. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet, or the authority where you live.

Deleting your data

In the app, Profile, then Account, then Erase all data (or Delete account if you signed in) removes everything Sorena keeps on your iPhone. Deleting the app does the same. Data already saved in Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app.

Children

Sorena is not directed at children under 13.

This website

This site is hosted without cookies, analytics or third-party fonts and scripts. The hosting provider may log visitors' IP addresses for security.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here and in the app.
